Island hand-off

The DOM pill above the sphere is the island; its rect is handed to setIslandPill(), so the shader starts exactly where the island is and hide() collapses back into it.

Backdrop refraction

Android feeds setBackdropBitmap() a blurred capture of the UI behind the sphere. A web canvas cannot read the page, so the demo paints a canvas-sized scene inside the canvas through setBackdrop(source, dynamic) — any image, <canvas> or <video> works.
